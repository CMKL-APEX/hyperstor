The trash (19-administration.md §23.9.3, 04-decisions.md D54): a deleted volume or snapshot keeps its data until it is purged, after a pool’s grace period or at once, or restored.

  • A trashed volume is a header in state VolumeState::Trashed with the time it went: still in its pool’s directory (so the purger finds it), not listed, not attachable (storage nodes grant no lease on a header that isn’t active), no snapshots taken of it. Trashing is refused as deleting is: while it is attached (unless forced), or while another volume reads one of its branches; so a purge can always go on.
  • A trashed snapshot has its time set: not listed, not cloned from or rolled back to. It is marked first and then checked for dependents, as a delete is, so a clone that recorded itself meanwhile is seen.
  • Purging is the delete of before: the volume delete that resumes after a crash, Client::delete_snapshot for a snapshot; a group’s object goes with its last snapshot.
  • Restoring clears the mark; a volume whose name another volume of the pool took meanwhile comes back as NAME-restored.
