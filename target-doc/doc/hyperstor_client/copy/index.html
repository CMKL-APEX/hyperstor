Copies and moves between pools (10-volumes.md §10.6, 04-decisions.md D214).

A copy reads a volume’s head (the volume held meanwhile, so nothing writes it) or one of its snapshots, and writes what it reads into a new volume in another pool, held too, skipping holes and blocks of zeros. The copy is flat: no snapshots, no parents. Pools may differ in chunk size: data is read and written by offset.

A move keeps the volume’s id. Its head is copied into lineage id of the new pool (free there: a lineage is named per pool), then its header points there in one compare-and-set, and the data left in the old pool is deleted. Before the switch, what is left behind is recorded in m/{id} in .meta, so a move that stops after it is finished by running it again. A move needs the volume detached, without snapshots, and with nothing reading through it; it stays held until the old data is gone.
