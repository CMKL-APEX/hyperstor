UdpSocket
A UDP socket, for peers’ pings (D185). Datagrams are small and few: a receive waits on the ring for the socket to be readable and then takes what arrived without blocking; a send that would block is refused at once, as a lost datagram would be.

KEEPALIVE_COUNT
KEEPALIVE_IDLE
TCP keepalive on every connection (01-implementation-notes.md I184): probes start after this long idle, every KEEPALIVE_INTERVAL, and KEEPALIVE_COUNT unanswered probes close it. A peer that vanished without a FIN or RST (a host gone, a network cut) is noticed in about 25 s instead of never.
KEEPALIVE_INTERVAL
USER_TIMEOUT
Data sent and not acknowledged for this long closes the connection (TCP_USER_TIMEOUT): keepalive covers idle connections, this one those with writes stuck in flight.

Functions§

bind_retrying
Binds addr, waiting up to BIND_RETRY while it is in use. A reactor’s listener stays open a moment after its process has exited: the kernel tears an io_uring instance down in the background, and a pending accept holds the socket until then. A service restarted at once (a test, systemctl restart) would otherwise find its own old address taken.
