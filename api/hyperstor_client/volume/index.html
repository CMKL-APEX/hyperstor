Module volume

Expand description

Volumes (06-metadata.md §4.2, 04-decisions.md D77): a header object per volume and directory objects per pool, in the .meta pool, changed by compare-and-set on the header’s version (01-implementation-notes.md I68).

  • Header h/{id} (32 hex digits): attributes v (the version, a decimal number), hdr (the encoded Volume), by (who created it: a token that recognises a retried create) and, while the volume is attached, lease (its holder, kept by the header’s primary: 06-metadata.md §9).
  • Directory d/{pool}/{shard}: the pool’s volumes in 64 shards by id, one key–value entry per volume (its id, big-endian).

A volume is identified by a 128-bit id, chosen by the caller (glidex: its disk’s UUID) or drawn at random; its name is shown, not unique.

Structs§

Attached
A volume attached through a Client: it holds the volume’s rw lease (06-metadata.md §9), which the client renews every second (D75), and does I/O within the volume. Once the lease is lost (taken over, its identity fenced, or this client blocklisted), I/O fails with ClientError::LeaseLost. Dropped without Attached::detach, its renewals stop: the lease runs out, and another instance must Client::take_over.
