pub fn bind_retrying(addr: SocketAddr) -> Result<TcpListener>
Expand description

Binds addr, waiting up to BIND_RETRY while it is in use. A reactor’s listener stays open a moment after its process has exited: the kernel tears an io_uring instance down in the background, and a pending accept holds the socket until then. A service restarted at once (a test, systemctl restart) would otherwise find its own old address taken.
